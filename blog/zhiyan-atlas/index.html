Zhiyan Atlas: Mapping China's Self-Driving Labs

Self-Driving Labs
Share X LinkedIn Bluesky

In August I published a world map of self-driving labs. Its companion, Zhiyan Atlas (智验图谱), covers the part of the picture that is hardest to see from outside: China. It lives at zhiyanatlas.com.

Why a separate atlas

Self-driving labs in China are advancing quickly. Because of language and platform barriers, much of this progress is hard for the global community to see, and the same holds the other way round. Zhiyan Atlas is a personal project to let peers in China and around the world see each other.

What it holds

As of 31 October 2026 the atlas lists 57 entries in 10 provinces and municipalities: Beijing 18, Shanghai 11, Zhejiang 9 and Guangdong 7, with the rest in Jiangsu, Anhui, Hubei, Tianjin, Liaoning and Fujian. By type there are 3 national laboratories, 12 new R&D institutes, 15 university and institute teams and 27 companies. Alongside them run 34 watch items: 17 on progress, 9 on funding and 8 policy documents.

A worldwide layer brings in the 103 entries outside China from the global map, translated into Chinese, so a reader in China sees both in one place.

How it is curated

The site states three rules. Public sources only: official websites, government documents, papers, press coverage and WeChat articles, each linked from its entry. No personal profiles: the atlas records institutions, platforms and policy. Neutral description: entries are described and filed without judgement, and the one test for inclusion is relevance to the self-driving lab ecosystem. A listed institution can claim its entry and add its own introduction and how it likes to collaborate; that text is labelled as provided by the institution.

Where to find it

The site is bilingual, Chinese first, at zhiyanatlas.com. The WeChat official account zhiyan_atlas posts updates, and a mini-program for phones is in review.

The aim

The mission I wrote in the first WeChat article: together with self-driving labs worldwide, to build a veritable interconnected community without boundary. Corrections and missing labs are welcome, through the site or the official account.
